Entanglement Lab

Two qubits are entangled when their joint state cannot be written as a product of two individual qubit states. Measuring one qubit then fixes the outcome of the other, no matter how far apart they are. Enter any two-qubit state below and the lab measures exactly how entangled it is.

03

The Four Bell States

Reference table

The Bell states are the four maximally entangled two-qubit states (C = 1). Together they form an orthonormal basis of the two-qubit state space, so any two-qubit state can be written as a combination of them. Select one to load it into the analyzer above.

Φ+
(|00⟩ + |11⟩) / √2

Even superposition of the two matching basis pairs.

Φ−
(|00⟩ − |11⟩) / √2

Matching pairs carrying a relative minus sign.

Ψ+
(|01⟩ + |10⟩) / √2

Even superposition of the two opposite pairs.

Ψ−
(|01⟩ − |10⟩) / √2

The singlet state — antisymmetric under qubit exchange.
