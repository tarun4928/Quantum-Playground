Understand the concepts behind the experiments in Quantum Playground.

01

What is a Qubit?

A qubit is the basic unit of quantum information. Unlike a classical bit, a qubit can exist in a combination of the states |0⟩ and |1⟩.

|ψ⟩ = α|0⟩ + β|1⟩
02

Quantum Gates

Quantum gates transform quantum states. Common gates include X, Y, Z and H.

New State = Gate × Old State
03

Measurement

Measuring a quantum state produces an outcome according to its probability distribution.

04

Entanglement

Entanglement creates strong quantum correlations between two or more qubits.

05

Bloch Sphere

The Bloch sphere provides a geometric way to visualize the state of a single qubit.

06

Quantum Circuits

Quantum circuits combine qubits and gates into sequences that perform quantum operations.
