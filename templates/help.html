User Guide

How to Use Quantum Playground

A quick guide to the different laboratories.

01

Single Qubit Explorer

Define arbitrary single-qubit states \(|\psi\rangle = \alpha|0\rangle + \beta|1\rangle\) or pick presets (like \(|0\rangle, |1\rangle, |+\rangle, |-\rangle\)), apply quantum gates, and observe rotations on the live 3D Bloch sphere.

02

Circuit Lab

Assemble sequences of quantum gates along qubit wires. Run the circuit step-by-step or all at once, and observe progressive state evolution and outcome probabilities.

03

Measurement Lab

Select the number of measurement shots (1 to 10,000) to simulate projective measurements and wave-function collapse, comparing theoretical probabilities with shot statistics.

04

Entanglement Lab

Prepare and analyze two-qubit systems. Create Bell states \(|\Phi^\pm\rangle, |\Psi^\pm\rangle\), compute concurrence, and observe correlated measurement outcomes.

05

Operator Lab

Enter custom 2 × 2 matrix operators and column vectors \(X\). Calculate matrix transformations \(AX\), verify unitarity \(A^\dagger A = I\), solve eigenvalues/eigenvectors, and inspect true 2D vector geometry.

06

Quantum State Challenge

Play interactive state-transformation challenges. Apply gates to navigate the Bloch sphere and reach the target state in the optimal number of moves.

07

Quantum Key Distribution (BB84)

Simulate optical photon key exchange between Kumar and Tarun using Rectilinear (+) and Diagonal (×) bases. Enable Eve to observe how eavesdropping disturbs qubits and injects measurable error rates.

08

Quantum Magnetometer

Observe Larmor spin precession in response to magnetic fields on the Bloch sphere, and estimate the magnetic field strength using quantum measurement statistics.
