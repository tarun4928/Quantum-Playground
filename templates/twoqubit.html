05

The Four Bell States

Reference table

The Bell states are the four maximally entangled two-qubit states (C = 1). Together they form an orthonormal basis of the two-qubit state space. Select one to load and analyze it.

Φ+
(|00⟩ + |11⟩) / √2

Even superposition of the two matching basis pairs.

Φ−
(|00⟩ − |11⟩) / √2

Matching pairs carrying a relative minus sign.

Ψ+
(|01⟩ + |10⟩) / √2

Even superposition of the two opposite pairs.

Ψ−
(|01⟩ − |10⟩) / √2

The singlet state — antisymmetric under qubit exchange.
